RULES

rank(r) = number of free indices= # of scalar field derivatives
order(s) = total order of differentiations of metric tensor(对g的导数)=i_Sum(λi+2)= mass_dimension
degree(q) = number of curvature tensor/scalar in one term
lambda(λ) = a set of numbers representing the order of derivative in each curvature tensor/scalar(q in total)
4-dimension = # of scalar field + # of coefficient dimension(这个怎么写是个问题)
# of Rank+ # of mass dimension <=6
